    Editions and allowances¶

    Vista Platform is one product in one set of images. What an install may do is decided by the licence it holds, not by which image it runs:

    • With no licence it runs as Vista Platform Core, free of charge, at a small allowance. Core is not a time-limited demo: the allowance is yours for as long as you run the install.
    • A 30-day trial (started from the admin console with your name, work email and company, in exchange for agreeing that Vista Security may contact you, see Starting your trial) turns on every capability at Pro's capacity. A trial is a state of Core, not a fifth edition: the console reads "Vista Platform Core, trial: 12 days left".
    • A paid licence makes the install Vista Platform Starter, Pro, Enterprise or MSP.

    Prices are on the website, vistasecurity.io, not in these pages. The platform is proprietary, free of charge for Core, under the Vista Platform License; the terms say which terms apply to what you download.

    What each edition allows¶

    Trial (30 days) Core Starter Pro Enterprise
    Assets 10,000 100 1,000 10,000 by agreement, above 10,000
    Sensors 50 1 10 50 unlimited
    Device agents 50 1 10 50 unlimited
    Users unlimited 3 unlimited unlimited unlimited
    Tenants 1 1 1 1 many
    History retention 2 years 90 days 1 year 2 years set by the platform administrator
    Every capability listed on Editions yes no yes yes yes
    A cold disaster-recovery standby and one non-production instance no no included included included, more by agreement
    Opt out of the usage report, custom terms, a support commitment no no no no by agreement
    • Every capability is in every paid edition. Single sign-on (OIDC), CMDB sync, the NetBox connector, SIEM export, the regulated frameworks (SOC 2, PCI-DSS, ISO 27001, NIST CSF, IEC 62351-3), custom policies, threshold overrides, CBOM signing and comparison, SPDX and PDF CBOM downloads, custom branding and your own AI provider are on in Starter, Pro and Enterprise alike. The editions differ in how much they hold. Core has none of these capabilities, but keeps its eight built-in frameworks. The full list, generated from the code that does the gating, is on Editions.
    • Everything not on that list is in every edition, Core included: sites, racks, cables, IPAM, circuits, the map, the cryptographic overlay, CycloneDX CBOM export, the built-in Platform Sensor, scheduled scans, the MCP server and the API.
    • The built-in Platform Sensor and platform device agent do not count against the sensor and device-agent allowance. The allowance is for the sensors and device agents you deploy on your own network.
    • Assets are the allowance that binds. Sensors and device agents scale so that nobody meets them before the asset allowance.
    • The retention period is not enforced in 6.0.0. The retention row is the period each edition is entitled to; nothing trims history to it yet.
    • Enterprise is distinguished by scale and contract: more than one tenant, capacity above Pro, instances beyond the two included, the usage-report opt-out, custom terms and a support commitment. Its price depends on the asset band, by quote.
    • MSP is for service providers who manage tenants for others. It is set up with Vista Security rather than started from the trial card, and its allowances come from the plans the provider defines. After 6.0 the offer will be revisited; for now, contact Vista Security.

    When an allowance is reached¶

    Allowance At the limit
    Assets Soft. An asset discovered over the allowance is kept but held: it appears in Discovery → Review → Approvals marked "over the Core allowance" (on a Core install) and is left out of lists, lenses, dashboards, the map, scopes, reports and exports. Nothing discovered is lost, and its evidence keeps accumulating. A licence that lifts the allowance admits everything.
    Sensors, device agents Hard. Enrolling one more is refused with a message that names the allowance.
    Users Hard. New invitations are refused. Nobody already signed up is locked out or removed.
    Tenants Hard. A second tenant needs Enterprise, or an exception on the licence.

    A licence can also change a single allowance for one install: an exception Vista Security grants, or an add-on you buy, such as more assets. It rides on the same licence token, so there is nothing else to install. See Installing and renewing a licence.

    What happens when a trial or a licence ends¶

    The install returns to the Core allowance. Nothing is deleted and sign-in is never blocked. The details, and how to choose which assets and agents stay active, are in Starting your trial.

    Reporting usage¶

    Every licensed install (a trial included) prepares a monthly usage report for Vista Security. Core reports nothing. What is in the report, and what is never in it, is on What is reported to Vista Security.
