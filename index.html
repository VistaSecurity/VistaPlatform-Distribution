    Customer Documentation¶

    Documentation for tenants and tenant admins — people who use Vista Platform to manage their organization's asset inventory and cryptographic posture.

    Quick Start¶

    I want to… Start here
    Install Vista Platform Core, or verify what I downloaded INSTALL.md and VERIFY.md in the Distribution repository; Deployment
    Go from first sign-in to a populated inventory Quick Start
    Get an overview of the platform Platform Overview
    Learn the vocabulary (asset, endpoint, class, relationship, …) Concepts
    Understand how the platform itself is secured Security Posture
    Learn the platform as an end user Tenant User Guide
    Administer my organization's settings Tenant Admin Guide
    Understand CBOM artifacts CBOM Artifacts
    Generate a software, hardware or full-inventory BOM, or export to a SIEM Bills of Materials

    Features¶

    The core surfaces, in the order a tenant meets them:

    • Concepts — the vocabulary: asset, endpoint, class, relationship, fact, finding, risk, alert, and more
    • Getting Started — the onboarding checklist
    • The Dashboard — the two heroes (cryptographic posture and assets monitored) and what each number means
    • Inventory & Lenses — the unified inventory and its switchable lenses
    • Networks, VLANs and IP addresses — the prefix tree, utilisation, free space, VLANs, VRFs and Find
    • Sites and locations — regions, site groups, sites and the locations inside them; placing assets; the site page
    • Racks and placement — racks, rack types and reservations; placing an asset at a unit and face; the placement rules
    • Cabling — cables, the faceplate, port traces, and LLDP/CDP proposals you approve
    • Power — power panels and feeds, power ports and outlets, power cables and their traces to the feed
    • Circuits — providers, circuits and their terminations
    • Virtualization — clusters, hosts, virtual machines and disks
    • VPN — tunnels and the IKE and IPsec policies behind them
    • Wireless — wireless LANs and links
    • Custom fields — your own validated fields on objects
    • Config contexts — JSON merged per asset
    • Saved filters and export templates
    • Changes and journal — who changed what, and dated notes
    • Business Units — nested owners on assets and prefixes
    • Contacts — contacts, roles, assignments and image attachments
    • Bulk import and export — CSV import with dry run and CSV export for every infrastructure object
    • Asset Classes & Identification Rules — the class taxonomy, identifier precedence, and the auto-accept threshold
    • Asset Approval
    • Asset Lifecycle Management
    • Assets and Crypto Configurations
    • Cryptographic Keys — the Keys lens: algorithm, size, lifecycle state, and where each key is used
    • Query — the one-line predicate the filter rail, saved views, scopes and approval rules all speak
    • Global Search — the ⌘K quick-find palette
    • The Map — two views: the neighbourhood graph around one asset (with the impact overlay and GraphML/Cytoscape export) and the tenant-wide topology tree
    • Relationships — how assets are connected, and what breaks if one changes
    • Interfaces — an asset's physical and virtual interfaces, LAGs and VLAN modes
    • Device types — the hardware catalogue, importing from the device-type library, and applying a type's interfaces to an asset
    • Spares and RMA — spare parts in stock, installing them, removing parts to stock, and return cases
    • Findings — what a finding is, who produces them, and what "open" means
    • Remediation — Alerts, the ticket Queue, and migration Plans
